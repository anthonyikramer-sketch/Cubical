Do not redesign the image.

--------------------------------------------------
1. ADD SAKURA AS A REAL SKIN
--------------------------------------------------

Create a new skin called:

Sakura

--------------------------------------------------
2. SAKURA HOME ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------

When Sakura is equipped, use the uploaded Sakura pixel-art image as the Home workspace environment.

The artwork should replace the normal plain Home workspace background.

Preserve the artwork's original appearance.

Do NOT:
- blur it
- stretch it disproportionately
- smooth the pixel art
- recolor it
- place a dark overlay over it
- cover it with another generic background

Use image-rendering behavior appropriate for pixel artwork where useful.

--------------------------------------------------
3. IMAGE SCALING
--------------------------------------------------

The Sakura environment needs to work across different Cubical window sizes.

Preserve the image's aspect ratio.

Prefer showing the entire environment whenever reasonably possible.

Do not distort the image to fill arbitrary dimensions.

The central Sakura tree, landscape, wooden frame, floor, bottom shelf, "You got this" note, and cat should remain visually coherent.

If the available workspace has a different aspect ratio, use intelligent containment/cropping rather than stretching.

The environment should feel intentionally framed inside Cubical.

--------------------------------------------------
